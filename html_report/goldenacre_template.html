What the data says to do next

1

Overall value sales are down 3.4% MAT vs. MAT YA (£2.52bn vs. £2.61bn) — but that understates the volume story. Unit sales fell 6.4%, while the average price per unit actually rose 3.2% (£2.00 → £2.07 MAT YA vs. MAT), which cushioned roughly half of the volume decline's impact on revenue. This is a genuine demand softening partly offset by price/mix, not a flat-price volume story.

2

Retailer performance is diverging. ASDA, the largest retailer at 47.5% share, saw the sharpest MAT decline (‑7.9%), while Morrisons (15.4% share) grew +7.7% — a share shift toward Morrisons worth investigating for distribution or space gains.

3

The single biggest lever available is data, not merchandising: 45.2% of MAT value sales (£1.14bn) sits in products with no product-reference match at all — larger than Halal and Polish combined (15.9%). This "Unclassified" bucket is also the only segment growing (+7.8%), while the reference-matched "Other" category shrank 14.0% — new SKUs appear to be entering faster than the reference master data is updated. Expanding reference-database coverage would sharpen every chart in this report, not just this one.

4

No single Halal brand has full 4-retailer strength. Haji Baba leads at ASDA (£28.8m), Tariq Halal at Sainsbury's (£21.9m), and Shazans leads at both Morrisons (£6.8m) and Tesco (£24.7m) — each retailer's shelf is led by a different brand, a plausible listing-expansion opportunity for whichever brand is currently under-distributed.

5

Short-term momentum (trailing 12 weeks) is broadly negative across almost every retailer and category — but fit quality is weak-to-moderate for most of them (e.g. the total trend's R² is just 0.10). Treat this as a real recent wobble worth watching, not a confident multi-month forecast; see the methodology note below.

Methodology

  • Source: GOLDENACRE.TRANSFORM.HC_MASTER joined to HC_PERIOD_DIM_CLEAN — Optia's harmonised layer, additive to and independent of Golden Acre's existing production pipeline (see multi-agents/docs/goldenacre/).
  • MAT (moving annual total) and its year-ago comparison (MAT YA) are ranked per retailer, not on a shared calendar — Tesco's week-ending date is offset by one day from the other three retailers every week, so a shared rank would misclassify everyone's current year.
  • "Predictions" are a linear trend slope over the trailing 12 weeks, normalised to %/week of that window's own mean — a directional signal, not a confidence-scored forecast. Fit quality (R²) is shown alongside every value so a noisy trend isn't read as a firm one.
  • "Unclassified" reflects products with no match in Golden Acre's product reference table as of its most recent snapshot — a data-coverage gap, not a business category. See the reference-match KPI above.
  • All figures were independently re-derived directly from Snowflake as a QA pass before delivery — see the note above.
